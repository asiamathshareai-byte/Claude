The method

Ability is given.
Excellence is practiced.

i.

Consistency

A mentor is present the moment homework begins, every evening. For an ADHD mind, showing up is half the work. We make it automatic.

ii.

Rigor

Each session stretches one problem past comfortable. When a student stalls, the problem is broken down, never handed over. They do the thinking.

iii.

Visibility

After every session you receive a clear account: what was attempted, what was corrected, what comes next. Effort you can see.

Core or Honors?

Core provides thirty mentored minutes each evening. Honors has no time limit and includes a written report after every session. Homework rarely ends at minute thirty.

Does the mentor give answers?

No. The mentor questions, prompts and breaks problems into steps. Your child does the work. That is the point.

What happens after the complimentary week?

Nothing automatic. There is no card on file. Choose a program when you are ready.
