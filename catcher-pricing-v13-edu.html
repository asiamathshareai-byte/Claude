The Learning Method

Four phases.
Every session.

Every session follows the same four steps, because for an ADHD brain, starting and staying are the hard part. The math comes after.

Warm up

The hardest problem is the first one. Every session opens with one they can win.

Stretch

One more. Then one more. A harder problem the moment they're ready for it.

Rethink

Stuck is part of learning. The tutor splits it into smaller steps. No sighing. No rushing.

Master

Every win logged. Every mistake they fixed themselves, reported to you.

Questions

Before you start.

Core or Honors?

Core gives your child thirty tutored minutes a day. Honors has no clock and sends you a report after every session. Homework rarely ends at minute thirty.

Does the tutor give answers?

No. The tutor asks, prompts and breaks problems down. Your child does the work. That is the point.

What happens on day seven?

Nothing automatic. There is no card on file. Choose a program when you are ready.

Do we need to schedule anything?

No. Your child opens Catcher when homework starts. A tablet with a stylus is required.

Can we pause?

Yes, anytime. If your child is inactive for 30 days, we pause the program for you.
