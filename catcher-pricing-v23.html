Do siblings get a discount?

Yes. Your second child is 30% off and every child after that is 50% off, on monthly and semester plans. Catcher for a second child is $140 a month.

Lite or Catcher?

Lite is text and handwriting, 20 hours a month. Catcher adds voice tutoring, has no clock, and sends you reports.

Do I schedule anything?

No. Open it when homework starts.

What happens on day 7?

Nothing automatic. There is no card on file. Pick a plan when you want to.

Can I pause?

Yes. If your kid stops for 30 days, we pause it for you.

Isn't this just ChatGPT?

ChatGPT answers questions. Ours talks out loud and watches the pen on the tablet.
