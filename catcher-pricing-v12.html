The Method

Four phases.
Every session.

Structured like a training session, because for an ADHD brain, starting and staying are the hard part. The math comes after.

Start

The hardest problem is the first one. Every session opens with one they can win.

Push

One more. Then one more. A harder problem the moment they're ready for it.

Recover

Stuck is part of training. The coach splits it into smaller steps. No sighing. No rushing.

Record

Every win logged. Every mistake they fixed themselves, reported to you.

Questions

Before you start.

Standard or All Access?

Standard gives your child thirty coached minutes a day. All Access has no clock and sends you a report after every session. Homework rarely ends at minute thirty.

Does the coach give answers?

No. The coach asks, prompts and breaks problems down. Your child does the work. That is the point.

What happens on day seven?

Nothing automatic. There is no card on file. Choose a membership when you are ready.

Do we need to schedule anything?

No. Your child opens Catcher when homework starts. A tablet with a stylus is required.

Can we pause?

Yes, anytime. If your child is inactive for 30 days, we pause the membership for you.
